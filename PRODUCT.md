# Premier Outdoor Solutions — product

- **Platform:** web
- **Stack:** static single-file HTML/CSS/JS (`index.html`), no frameworks, no tracking, system fonts
- **Surface mode:** Persuade (pitch one-pager)

## Users
1. **The owner** of Premier Outdoor Solutions (name unknown), who will open the link from a Facebook message on a phone. The page has to show them their own business, done better than they imagined, in one screen.
2. **Their customers:** homeowners, landlords and contractors in and around Middlesex County, NJ, who need junk gone. Often on a phone, often inside Facebook's in-app browser.

## Purpose
Turn "I have stuff I need gone" into a free-estimate request in under a minute, using the owner's own process: *send a picture and we'll let you know.*

## Positioning
A new, local, eager junk removal crew: fair pricing, local and reliable, flexible scheduling, heavy lifting included. Their own words: "We're a new local junk removal business looking to help out homeowners, landlords, contractors, and anyone who just needs some extra space."

## Capabilities and constraints
- **Contact today:** Facebook Messenger only (page ID 61572063225847). No phone, no email published. Primary action = `m.me/61572063225847` (opens Messenger; works in Facebook's in-app browser).
- Phone/text buttons cannot be wired until the owner gives a number: shown as a visible "coming soon" gap, not invented.
- No prices, reviews, photos of work, hours or exact towns exist; the page says nothing about them.

- Logo (from their Facebook profile image): black roundel, lime-green brush "JUNK" + white "REMOVAL", white box truck piled with furniture, green recycle symbol. Colors: lime green (~#5fd13a), white, black.
